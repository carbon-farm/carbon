Organic Carbon Farming

Phase 1 platform flow

A clean, structured version of the platform journey — from a farmer opening the app, through the Farmer Dashboard's modules, into the ten-state Case Lifecycle, and finally into the separate, Moderator-gated workflow that turns a closed case into public knowledge. Built to reflect the Phase 1 scope in the Project Charter: fully human-expert-operated, no AI in the Case Management path.

Diagram 1 — Overview

Farmer journey & module map

Every member enters through registration or login, lands on a single dashboard, and branches into five modules. Notifications and Administration/Moderation run underneath all of them.

Flowchart: farmer registers or logs in, arrives at the Farmer Dashboard, then branches into Learning, Report a Case, Soil Laboratory, Marketplace, and Knowledge Repository. Notifications and Administration run as platform-wide services beneath all modules.

Diagram 2 — Detail

Case lifecycle

Case Management, modeled on CRM ticketing: a farmer reports a Disease, Pest, Nutrient Deficiency, Weather Damage, an Unknown Problem, or a General Advisory/Planning question, and the Case moves through ten defined states. It can loop between expert and farmer, be disputed and Reopened instead of confirmed, or auto-close as Abandoned if the farmer goes quiet — Closed is the end of the Case's own lifecycle (what happens next is Diagram 3).

Flowchart: a case moves through Draft, Submitted, Under Review, and Assigned, then alternates between Expert Working and Waiting Farmer until the expert is ready, then reaches Answered, where the farmer either confirms (proceeding to Farmer Confirmed then Closed) or disputes it (moving to Reopened, which loops back to Expert Working). A case left waiting on the farmer past its SLA window auto-closes as Abandoned.

Diagram 3 — Detail

Knowledge Article publication workflow

A separate workflow from the Case Lifecycle, deliberately: closing a case never publishes anything by itself. Every draft passes a Moderator before it can pollute — or improve — the searchable Knowledge Repository.

Flowchart: a closed case generates a draft knowledge article, which a moderator approves, after which it is published and visible in the knowledge repository.

Reflects the Phase 1 scope defined in 000-Project-Charter.md (v0.3.0) — no AI in the Case Management path; every case is worked and resolved by a named human expert, and every published article passes a Moderator. Marketplace and Case Management are shown as separate branches from the Farmer Dashboard, consistent with the charter's Advisory Independence principle.
